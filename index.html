About us

Engineers first. Salespeople second.

ATESTAKI ENGINEERING NIG LTD is a Nigerian electrical engineering company built around one idea: power infrastructure has to work, every single time. We plan every transformer installation, panel build and wiring job the way we'd want it done for our own facility — properly earthed, properly rated, and properly documented.
